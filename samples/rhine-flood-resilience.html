Upper Rhine Flood Resilience Programme

Fictional demonstration document. Published by the Upper Rhine Water Partnership (Fictional) for North Rhine-Westphalia on 5 November 2024.

Programme objective

The programme combines embankment maintenance, natural flood storage, and improved warning systems for five fictional riverside communities. Its objective is to reduce disruption from frequent flood events while retaining space for the river during exceptional flows.

Priority works

Engineers will repair erosion damage along 7.5 kilometres of embankment and replace fourteen inspection gates. Two former gravel sites will be adapted as controlled flood-storage areas. Native wetland planting will stabilise exposed banks and create habitat between inspection tracks and the river.

Community preparedness

Water-level sensors will send observations every five minutes to the regional warning platform. Municipal teams will run annual exercises covering road closures, care-home support, temporary shelters, and communication with residents who do not use mobile alerts.

Schedule and outcomes

Surveys start in January 2025, construction begins after the summer ecological window, and the first storage area is expected to operate in winter 2026. Reported outcomes will include storage capacity created, warning lead time, assets inspected, and hectares of restored wetland.
