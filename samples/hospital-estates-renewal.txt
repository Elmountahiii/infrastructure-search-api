SAXONY COMMUNITY HOSPITAL ESTATES RENEWAL PROGRAMME
Fictional demonstration document

Source: Saxony Public Estates Unit (Fictional)
Publication date: 2024-09-30

Programme overview

The estates renewal programme covers four fictional community hospitals in Saxony. It prioritises reliable heating, electrical safety, water hygiene, and clinical areas that can be refurbished without reducing emergency capacity.

Energy and utilities

Old steam boilers at Falkenstadt Hospital will be replaced by a staged low-temperature heating system. Sonnenberg Hospital will receive a new electrical ring main and emergency generator controls. Smart meters will provide separate energy data for wards, diagnostic services, kitchens, and administration buildings.

Clinical infrastructure

Ventilation upgrades will improve air-change control in treatment rooms. Ageing nurse-call systems will be replaced, and redundant network routes will connect diagnostic equipment to the regional clinical platform. Works near occupied wards will use dust monitoring and scheduled isolation plans.

Water resilience

Each site will map seldom-used outlets, install additional temperature monitoring, and replace sections of corroded distribution pipe. A cross-site emergency plan will identify tanker connection points and minimum potable-water requirements.

Phasing

Survey and design work runs through 2025. Utility replacements begin in early 2026, followed by clinical refurbishments through 2027. The programme will publish outage hours, safety defects resolved, energy use per square metre, and completed ward phases.
