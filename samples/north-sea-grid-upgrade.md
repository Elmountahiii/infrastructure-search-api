# North Sea Regional Grid Reinforcement Plan

> Fictional demonstration document published by the Coastal Energy Coordination Office (Fictional).

**Region:** Schleswig-Holstein  
**Publication date:** 2025-03-18

## Purpose

The fictional North Sea grid programme prepares the regional electricity network for new offshore wind connections, community solar generation, and growing industrial demand. It focuses on resilience and capacity without assuming that every proposed generation project will proceed.

## Substation programme

Three substations at Nordhafen, Seewalde, and Marschfeld will receive additional transformers and modern protection systems. The programme reserves space for later battery storage while limiting construction to previously developed land where practical.

## Transmission corridors

The preferred route uses existing transport and utility corridors for most of its length. Underground cable will be assessed near protected landscapes and dense settlements. Final route selection will follow technical surveys and fictional public consultation.

## Reliability measures

New monitoring equipment will identify voltage instability and equipment overheating earlier. Mobile transformers will be stationed at two depots during construction so that planned maintenance does not create a single point of failure.

## Delivery milestones

- Detailed design: April to December 2025
- Procurement: January to June 2026
- Enabling works: July 2026
- Main construction: 2027 to 2028
- Operational review: March 2029

The programme will report added connection capacity, outage minutes avoided, and the percentage of critical equipment covered by condition monitoring.
