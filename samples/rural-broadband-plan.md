# Rural Broadband Access Plan 2025–2028

_Fictional demonstration document from the Hesse Digital Access Office (Fictional)._

## Context

Small settlements in the fictional eastern district have inconsistent fixed broadband and limited mobile coverage. The programme establishes a shared infrastructure plan so that road authorities, utilities, and network operators can coordinate civil works.

## Coverage priorities

The first phase covers thirty-two villages with schools, medical practices, or business parks that currently lack a reliable high-capacity connection. The second phase covers scattered premises where conventional street cabinets would not be economical.

## Delivery model

Open-access fibre ducts will be installed alongside planned water and road renewals. Neutral connection chambers will allow multiple operators to use the civil infrastructure under published terms. Fixed wireless links may be used as an interim service where fibre construction requires additional permits.

## Resilience and maintenance

Backbone routes will avoid a single shared river crossing. Critical cabinets will include battery backup and remote environmental monitoring. Asset records will use a common coordinate system so that emergency repair teams can locate chambers without relying on operator-specific maps.

## Measures of success

The fictional authority will publish premises passed, active connections, average restoration time, and kilometres of reusable duct. The plan targets 95 percent eligible-premises coverage by December 2028.
